# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Ryle Anthony Gabotero for software-engineering roles. Secondary users are potential collaborators, clients, and technical peers reviewing his work, capabilities, and professional record.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio helps visitors assess Ryle's engineering capabilities and supporting evidence, then reach him through an appropriate professional channel. Success means generating qualified software-engineering opportunities, client inquiries, or credible collaboration opportunities.

## Positioning

An evidence-backed engineering portfolio connecting backend systems, cybersecurity, client software development, and applied AI. Claims are supported through working projects, source repositories, competition records, credentials, demonstrations, and activity evidence rather than an unsupported skills list.

## Operating Context

Visitors move between a profile overview, filterable project case studies, experience and credentials, an evidence gallery, and contact options. They may arrive through search, a shared profile link, or a direct link to a specific project or piece of evidence. The experience must support quick scanning as well as deeper technical evaluation across desktop and mobile browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Presents six categorized engineering projects with technical descriptions, technology lists, live previews, repositories, demonstrations, and related evidence where available.
- Documents shipped client work, competition experience, training, assessments, credentials, and supporting gallery material.
- Provides professional profile links and a contact workflow backed by Web3Forms.
- Uses Next.js App Router, React, and TypeScript, with canonical metadata, sitemap, robots metadata, and structured person data.
- Must remain responsive, theme-aware, keyboard-accessible, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- All project, achievement, credential, and capability claims must remain verifiable. Future work must not invent clients, outcomes, benchmarks, testimonials, awards, or other proof.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the personal identity `Ryle Anthony Gabotero` and the `devR6` wordmark.
- Preserve the established professional profile and contact links unless Ryle explicitly updates them.
- Keep the voice direct, technically specific, credible, and evidence-led.
- Present Ryle as a software engineer spanning backend systems, full-stack client development, cybersecurity, applied AI, and data engineering without overstating expertise or outcomes.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with demonstrable work and connect every important claim to available proof.
2. Make technical breadth easy to scan without flattening meaningful implementation detail.
3. Help evaluators move quickly from identity to capability, evidence, and contact.
4. Preserve factual precision and professional credibility over promotional exaggeration.
5. Keep the portfolio usable and coherent across routes, themes, viewport sizes, input methods, and motion preferences.
